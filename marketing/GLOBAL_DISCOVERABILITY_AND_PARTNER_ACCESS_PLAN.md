# CASHUP — Global discoverability and partner access plan

Status: planning document. No profiles, accounts, domains, mailboxes, listings, outreach campaigns or paid services have been activated by this document.

## Objective

Make CASHUP easy for legitimate merchants, brands, affiliate networks, agencies, advertisers, publishers and strategic partners worldwide to discover, understand and contact. Build both inbound discovery and disciplined outbound business development.

The goal is broad, relevant international coverage—not indiscriminate mass-submission. Every profile must be accurate, maintained and consistent.

## 1. One canonical identity

Before creating external listings, approve and standardize:
- Brand spelling: CASHUP
- Slogan: IL MOVIMENTO PREMIA
- Short company/product description in Italian and English
- Official website and partner landing-page URL
- Official business contact email and a monitored fallback
- Social profile handles and logo/avatar variants
- Country/legal operator details only when verified and appropriate
- Public product status: in development; features and commercial availability must not be overstated

Do not invent a legal company name, registered office, VAT number, phone number, audience, traffic, users, revenue, partners or customer testimonials.

## 2. Contact architecture

Recommended role-based addresses on a domain controlled by CASHUP:
- partners@ — affiliate and merchant partnerships
- advertising@ — sponsored placements, media and campaigns
- press@ — media and public relations
- support@ — user support (only when support operations are ready)
- privacy@ — privacy requests (only when the responsible contact/process is established)
- hello@ — general contact

Start with one monitored mailbox and aliases/forwarding if the selected provider supports it. Do not buy a domain or mailbox until the exact domain, renewal price, email deliverability, account ownership and recovery method are reviewed and approved by the owner. A provider subdomain or free forwarding is not equivalent to owning a branded domain and full mailbox.

## 3. Global discovery channels

### Core company and professional presence
- Official website and bilingual partner page
- LinkedIn company page and founder/company professional presence
- Official Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other social profiles where a real content plan can be maintained
- Consistent brand profiles and contact details

### Search, maps and business data
- Search engine indexing, sitemap, robots rules, structured organization/product data and clear contact pages
- Bing and other relevant search webmaster tools
- Apple/Bing/Google business listings only when the actual business and location meet each platform's eligibility rules. Online-only projects must not create ineligible local storefront listings.
- Relevant business data providers and directories after checking their source, accuracy and correction process

### Startup, software and app discovery
- Product launch communities and startup directories
- Software/app directories and review sites whose categories genuinely fit CASHUP
- AI directories only for real, working CASHUP AI features—not roadmap concepts
- Developer and founder communities where participation is useful and permitted

### Commerce and affiliate ecosystem
- Affiliate networks and publisher marketplaces
- Cashback, loyalty, coupon, comparison and shopping partnership ecosystems
- Direct merchant/brand partnership portals
- Advertising, sponsorship and media-buying marketplaces
- Sector-specific networks (travel, food, fashion, electronics, finance, home, mobility, etc.) when the offer is relevant and legally permitted

### Editorial and relationship channels
- Relevant trade publications, podcasts, newsletters, events and industry associations
- Agency, PR, influencer and creator collaborations
- Reciprocal content, newsletter and banner exchanges where terms and value are clear

## 4. Prioritization and quality control

Score each candidate channel before submission:
1. Audience and geographic relevance
2. Fit for a digital consumer platform and/or affiliate publisher
3. Access to merchants, brands or users
4. Cost, renewal and hidden conditions
5. Verification and legal requirements
6. Reputation, moderation and spam risk
7. Time required to maintain the listing
8. Ability to measure referral traffic and commercial outcomes

Use three tiers:
- Tier A: essential, credible, free or low-cost channels with clear fit
- Tier B: relevant industry or country-specific channels
- Tier C: experimental channels; use only after evidence of value

Do not pay for directory placement, badges, backlinks or expedited reviews without a separate cost/benefit review and explicit owner approval. Avoid mass-submission services, link schemes, fake reviews, bought followers and unsolicited bulk email.

## 5. Inbound partner journey

Every channel should lead to one canonical partner page with:
- Partnership types and clear value proposition
- Honest statement of current product and program readiness
- Country/sector coverage and any limitations
- Official email contact and, when implemented safely, a secure inquiry form
- Privacy notice and clear data-use explanation before collecting personal data
- A response process and expected response window only once it can be staffed

Partner inquiries should be categorized as affiliate/performance, direct merchant, advertising/sponsorship, co-marketing, strategic partnership, press or other.

## 6. Outbound partner development

Build verified target lists by country, vertical and partner type. Prioritize:
- Affiliate networks with relevant international coverage
- Merchants with clear affiliate or partnership programs
- Brands with suitable products and legitimate advertising budgets
- Agencies and media networks able to introduce multiple merchants
- Local partners who can support market-specific requirements

For each prospect record source, official URL, market, vertical, contact route, eligibility, commercial model, date checked, status, owner, next action and outcome. Contact only appropriate business channels, personalize messages, respect opt-outs and local marketing/privacy laws, and do not send bulk unsolicited messages.

## 7. Commercial integrity

- Never claim traffic, user numbers, conversions, sales or partner logos without evidence and permission.
- Separate CASHUP revenue from pending/approved affiliate commissions and user cashback liabilities.
- Cashback must be expressly authorized by the merchant/network, funded by verified commission and payable only under clear approval/return-window rules.
- Sponsored placements must be labeled and must not override user value or editorial integrity.
- Review product quality, merchant reliability, terms, prohibited sectors, market eligibility, returns, attribution, data handling and support capability before activation.

## 8. Measurement

Track by channel, country and campaign:
- Verified impressions and visits
- Partner-page visits and qualified inquiries
- Partner response and acceptance rates
- Applications submitted, accepted and activated
- Clicks, approved conversions, cancellations and commission
- Sponsored campaign revenue and delivery
- User benefit/cashback liability
- Cost, staff time, contribution margin and net profit

Do not use vanity counts as proof of commercial success.

## 9. Phased execution

### Phase 0 — Identity and readiness
Approve canonical identity, domain/email decision, bilingual copy, contact ownership, privacy/legal basics and truthful product status.

### Phase 1 — Inbound foundations
Publish and test the partner page, create official social/company profiles, set up monitored email and establish inquiry handling.

### Phase 2 — High-value discovery
Submit to the most relevant affiliate networks, startup/software directories and industry communities; verify each platform's current rules and fees.

### Phase 3 — International outbound
Build country/vertical prospect lists and conduct personalized outreach in batches; log all replies and follow-ups.

### Phase 4 — Scale what works
Expand to more countries and channels based on qualified inquiries, partner activation, verified revenue and net contribution—not listing count.

## 10. Owner approvals required

The following remain owner decisions and must not be taken automatically:
- Buying or renewing a domain, mailbox or paid service
- Choosing a legal company identity or publishing registration/tax details
- Creating accounts that require acceptance of terms or personal/legal information
- Sending external outreach or publishing claims on behalf of CASHUP
- Signing agreements, granting exclusivity, accepting commissions or paying fees
- Collecting partner personal data before privacy/security controls are ready

## Current state

This is a plan, not proof of execution. The partner landing page is a draft on the documentation branch. No global directory profile, partner account, mailbox, domain, affiliate network account, outreach campaign or paid promotion is represented as active.
